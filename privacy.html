Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

EcoWise Junk Removal ("EcoWise", "we") serves Austin, TX and surrounding areas. This page explains what information we collect on ecowisejunkremovall.com and how we use it.

Information you give us

  • Your name, phone number, email and pickup address when you request a quote or book a pickup.
  • Photos and descriptions of the items you want removed.
  • Messages you send to our assistant, JARVIS.

How we use it

  • To prepare your quote, schedule your appointment and contact you about your pickup.
  • Photos and messages are processed by an AI service (Google Gemini) to estimate the size of your load and answer your questions.
  • Appointments are saved to our Google Calendar and quote details are emailed to our team.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the business (such as Google and our website host, Netlify), or when required by law.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site (for example, pages visited). It uses cookies. You can block cookies in your browser settings or use the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may call or text you about your quote or appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts.

How long we keep it

We keep quote and booking information only as long as needed to provide our service and for normal business records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time by calling or texting (512) 369-2644 or emailing ecowisejunkremovall@gmail.com.
