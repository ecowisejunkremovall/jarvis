Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be present for the pickup?

Not always — if items are accessible (garage, curb, driveway) we can often complete the job without you present. Just coordinate details with us ahead of time.

What items don't you take?

We're not able to take hazardous materials (chemicals, paint, asbestos) or certain regulated items. If you're not sure, just send us a photo and we'll let you know.

How fast can you come?

Most jobs are scheduled same-day or next-day, depending on your location and our schedule that day.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash, credit/debit cards, Zelle, and Venmo. Payment is collected after the job is complete.

Do you recycle or donate items?

Yes — sorting, donating usable items, and recycling responsibly is core to how we operate, not an afterthought.
