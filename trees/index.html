FAQ

Questions

What does lawn service include?

Every visit: mow, edge sidewalks, driveway and beds, string-trim around fences and trees, and blow clippings off hard surfaces (never into the street). We send before and after photos.

How do I pay for lawn service?

You save a card once and it is charged on the day of each visit. No contracts — cancel anytime with 7 days' notice. Rain moves the visit to the next dry day.

Do I need a City permit?

In Austin, trees 19 inches or more in diameter (measured 4.5 ft above ground) are protected, and some species are heritage trees. We tell you if your tree likely needs a permit and help with the paperwork.

Is the AI price the final price?

It’s our real price based on your photos. We confirm it on a free visit, because things like power lines, access and what’s under the tree can change the work.

Do you haul everything away?

Yes. Logs, branches and brush are chipped or hauled to a recycling site — we leave the yard clean.

Do you work near power lines?

Trees touching power lines must be handled by a line-clearance qualified crew. We coordinate it and handle the clean-up.
