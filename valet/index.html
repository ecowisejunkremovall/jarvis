Austin compliance

Help with Austin’s Universal Recycling Ordinance

Austin communities with five or more units must provide recycling and (since October 1, 2024) composting, post bilingual signs, educate residents, and submit an annual recycling plan between October 1 and February 1. Violations can be fined up to $2,000 per day.

Source: City of Austin — Multifamily Recycling and Composting Requirements

  • Bilingual resident education — English/Spanish rules flyer for move-ins, program changes and the yearly reminder, with a dated log.
  • Recycling that gets recycled — separate bags at the door, carried to your recycling container, contamination reported.
  • Records for your annual plan — doors served, trash and recycling bags, violations with photos.
  • Container areas ready for inspection — overflow, broken equipment and missing signs reported the same night.

Your recycling and compost containers and their pickup stay with your licensed hauler; EcoWise moves residents’ bags to them and supports your education and records.

Residents

How valet trash works at your community

Set out bagged trash 6–8 pm on your community’s service nights; porters collect 8–11 pm. Bring the can in by 10 am the next day. Questions about your service nights? Contact your leasing office.

Rules

  • Trash always inside the valet can — never on the ground.
  • Tie your bag. Leaking or ripped bags are not collected.
  • Maximum 50 lb per bag. Break down boxes.
  • Double-bag pet waste.
  • No furniture, mattresses, appliances, TVs, construction debris, paint, batteries or sharp items.
  • Recycling (where offered) goes in a separate bag — clean, empty containers only.

Items that don’t follow the rules may not be collected, based on your community guidelines, and are reported to the office.

Reglas

  • La basura siempre dentro del bote de valet — nunca en el piso.
  • Amarra tu bolsa. Las bolsas rotas o que gotean no se recogen.
  • Máximo 50 libras por bolsa. Desarma las cajas.
  • Desechos de mascotas en doble bolsa.
  • No se recogen muebles, colchones, electrodomésticos, televisiones, escombro, pintura, baterías ni objetos filosos.
  • El reciclaje (donde aplica) va en una bolsa aparte — solo envases limpios y vacíos.

Lo que no siga las reglas puede no recogerse, según las reglas de tu comunidad, y se reporta a la oficina.

FAQ

Questions from property managers

How is the price set?

By the number of homes, service nights, doors up stairs and whether recycling is included. You get a written proposal after a short walk of the property. The first month is due at signing, then service is billed monthly in advance.

Who provides the valet cans?

The community provides the cans for each home. We can recommend sizes and quantities before launch.

Do we have to take service five nights a week?

No. Choose the nights that fit your community; you can change them in writing.

What happens with violations?

Porters photograph violations with the door number and report them to your office the same night. We don’t knock on doors or confront residents.

Is there an extra charge for stairs?

Doors up a staircase without an elevator take longer, so they carry a small per-door surcharge shown clearly in your proposal.
